Math & Research

I am interested in various parts of mathematics, physics and computer science. Currently I am mostly looking into applied mathematics with connections to physics and biology.

My bachelor's thesis, supervised by Prof. Herbert Koch, is about DiPerna-Lions theory and a recent example for nonuniqueness of the transport equation with rough coefficients.

Some years ago, I worked on parallelizing Pollard's rho algorithm for integer factorization and created a visualization of the rho method.
